Our approach to food

Freshly prepared, shared together

Nutritious, freshly prepared meals are cooked with care each day and balanced for growing children. Mealtimes are shared family-style — a calm, social part of the day that builds independence and good habits. We cater warmly for allergies and individual dietary needs, working closely with every family so each child is happy, healthy and well-nourished throughout their day with us.

At Hammersmith, meals are supported through calm, social mealtimes and balanced snacks, with the same careful attention to allergies and dietary needs.

How we plan a balanced plate

Guided by official early-years nutrition standards

Our menus are planned in line with the UK Government's "Eat Better, Start Better" voluntary food and drink guidance for early years settings (Department for Education, gov.uk). In practice, this means every meal draws from the five food groups:

🥦

Fruit & vegetables

🍞

Starchy carbohydrates

🍗

Protein

🧀

Dairy & alternatives

🫒

Healthy oils, used sparingly

  • Fresh drinking water available at all times
  • Milk or water offered as the only drinks with meals and snacks
  • No added sugar or salt, and minimal processed food
  • Portions and menus adapted by age, appetite and stage of weaning
  • Cultural, religious and personal dietary preferences respected and planned for

Source: "Eat Better, Start Better" early years food & drink guidance, Department for Education / gov.uk.

Allergen & dietary care

Keeping every child safe at mealtimes

We take allergies seriously and follow current UK food-labelling law, including "Natasha's Law", which covers the 14 major allergens. Please tell us about any allergy, intolerance or dietary requirement — including cultural or religious preferences — as part of registration, and we will plan your child's meals accordingly with the room team and kitchen.

Source: Food Standards Agency (food.gov.uk) — allergen labelling guidance for schools, colleges and nurseries.
