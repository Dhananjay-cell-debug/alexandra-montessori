# Alexandra Montessori - photographic plates (jobs B)

You are producing photographic background plates for Instagram posts for Alexandra Montessori,
a small, premium Montessori nursery group in West London (Hounslow, Heston, Hammersmith).
Typography and logo are added later by someone else - the plates must contain NO text.

## How to work
- Use your built-in `image_gen` tool. One call per job. Portrait orientation, the largest
  portrait size available (e.g. 1024x1536). Highest quality.
- After each image is generated, copy the file from `$CODEX_HOME/generated_images/...` into
  `./plates/` with the exact filename given in the job (PNG). Do not overwrite an existing
  file; if the name is taken, append `-v2`, `-v3`.
- Look at each result. If it breaks any rule in "Global rules" (text, extra fingers, visible
  child face, plastic/CGI look, wrong layout space), regenerate once before moving on.
- Do nothing else: no code, no other files.

## Global rules (apply to every job)
- Editorial documentary photography, as if shot by a high-end UK interiors/lifestyle
  photographer for a magazine feature. Full-frame camera, 50mm lens, around f/2.8, soft
  natural daylight from a large north-facing window. Natural colour, gentle fine film grain,
  slightly lifted blacks, true-to-life skin. Feels real, unstaged, quiet.
- Palette: oat, linen, warm white, pale birch/beech wood, soft sage and deep laurel-green
  accents. Avoid saturated primary colours and plastic toys.
- Real-world imperfection: faint water drops, wood grain, fabric texture, a little wear.
- ABSOLUTELY NO identifiable children's faces. Children appear only as hands, forearms,
  sleeves, or from behind / cropped below the eyes. Anatomically correct small hands with
  five fingers each.
- No text, letters, numbers, logos, signage, labels or watermarks anywhere in the image.
- Not stock-photo, not glossy, not CGI, not "AI-looking". No bokeh balls, no lens flare,
  no HDR.
- Leave the calm negative space exactly where each job asks - typography will sit there.
