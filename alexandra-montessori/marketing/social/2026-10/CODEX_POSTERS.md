# Alexandra Montessori - complete Instagram promotional posters

Design and generate FINISHED promotional posters (complete graphic design: headline, copy,
hero child, decorative elements, CTA button, footer with branches) for Alexandra Montessori,
a Montessori nursery group in West London, UK (babies to 5 years). These are for Instagram
and Facebook, aimed at London parents. They must look like the work of a top UK branding
agency: far more refined, considered and premium than typical nursery flyers - but still
warm, joyful and instantly readable, exactly like a promotional poster.

## How to work
- Use your built-in `image_gen` tool, one call per poster, highest quality.
  Size: 4:5 portrait (1024x1280 if supported; otherwise the closest portrait size and
  compose for 4:5).
- Copy each result from `$CODEX_HOME/generated_images/...` into `./full/` with the exact
  filename given (append `-v2` if it exists).
- QC every poster at full size. ALL text must be spelled exactly as given in the brief,
  every digit of every phone number exact, no extra invented words, no gibberish small text.
  Also check hands/fingers/faces of children. If anything is wrong, regenerate (up to 2
  more tries) and keep the best.
- Do nothing else: no code, no other files.

## BRAND SYSTEM (identical on every poster - this is a series)
- Background: warm cream #F7F2EA with a very faint hand-drawn laurel-leaf line pattern.
- Colours: deep laurel green #2F4A35 (headlines, footer bar), sage #A3BC9A, pale sage
  #E6EEE1, warm apricot #E8875A (accent word, highlights), butter yellow #F2CF6B (CTA
  button), blush #F2B8B0. No rainbow clutter, no neon, no primary red/blue.
- Headline type: a chunky, soft, rounded high-contrast display SERIF (like Cooper / Fraunces
  Black) in laurel green, with ONE accent word in apricot italic. Body text: clean geometric
  sans (like Poppins), dark green-grey.
- Logo: TOP-LEFT, leave a plain white circle (about 13% of poster width) with a soft
  shadow and NOTHING inside it - the real logo is placed there afterwards. Do not draw any
  logo or brand name anywhere else.
- Signature motif: a tall rounded ARCH shape (Montessori arched window) in sage or apricot
  behind the hero child, with a thin dashed inner outline.
- Decorative accents: a few soft matte 3D clay-style Montessori objects in the palette
  (pink tower cubes, wooden rainbow stacker, golden bead bar, small globe, laurel sprig),
  plus a delicate dotted hand-drawn path. Tasteful - 2 to 4 accents max, never stars, paper
  planes, trophies or cartoon clutter.
- Hero child: photographic, high-end UK children's catalogue studio photography, cleanly
  cut out, natural joyful expression, real skin texture, age 2-4 (this is a nursery, not a
  school). Simple knitwear/cotton in sage, oat, cream, rust, butter. NO school uniform,
  NO logos on clothes. London-diverse.
- FOOTER on every poster: a full-width deep laurel green bar along the bottom (~9% of
  height) with three equal columns separated by thin light dividers, each with a small
  butter-yellow map-pin icon, the branch name in small uppercase sage letters and the
  phone number in white bold:
    HOUNSLOW  0208 001 5165   |   HESTON  0203 627 6707   |   HAMMERSMITH  0204 618 3477
- Top-right on every poster, small laurel text: alexandramontessori.co.uk
- Generous margins, clear hierarchy, nothing touching the edges.
