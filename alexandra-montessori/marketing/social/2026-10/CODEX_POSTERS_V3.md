# Alexandra Montessori - poster set v3 (MINIMAL, VERIFIED COPY)

Finished Instagram promotional posters for Alexandra Montessori, a Montessori nursery group
in West London (Hounslow, Heston, Hammersmith). Previous versions were liked for their
concepts and elements but felt CLUTTERED. v3 = the same ideas, made calm, minimal and
premium - like a high-end UK brand (think Aesop / Liberty / a design-led nursery), still warm
and instantly clear as a promotional poster.

## How to work
- Built-in `image_gen`, one call per poster, highest quality, 4:5 portrait (1024x1280 if
  supported, else closest portrait and compose for 4:5).
- Save each result from `$CODEX_HOME/generated_images/...` into `./full_v3/` with the exact
  filename (append `-v2` if taken).
- QC at full size: the text must match the brief WORD FOR WORD and DIGIT FOR DIGIT - no extra
  words, no invented small print, no misspellings. Clean hands/faces. Not cluttered. If any
  check fails, regenerate (max 2 more tries) and keep the best.
- Nothing else: no code, no other files.

## MINIMAL SYSTEM (strict, every poster)
- Background: plain warm cream #F7F2EA. NO all-over leaf pattern. Lots of empty space.
- Margins: at least 7% on every side. Nothing crammed; generous gaps between blocks.
- Hierarchy: ONE hero image, ONE headline (max 2 lines), at most ONE short supporting line,
  at most THREE short info items, ONE CTA. Every word is listed below - add nothing.
- Decoration: at most ONE small soft-matte 3D Montessori object (as named) per poster. No
  dotted paths, no sprigs scattered around, no stars or confetti.
- Colours: deep laurel #2F4A35 (headline, footer), sage #A3BC9A / pale sage #E6EEE1, apricot
  #E8875A for the one accent word, butter #F2CF6B for the CTA button only.
- Type: headline in a soft, chunky high-contrast display serif (Cooper/Fraunces-like) in laurel
  with the accent word in apricot italic; everything else in a clean geometric sans
  (Poppins-like), modest sizes, sentence case.
- Hero arch: the hero child/photo sits in or in front of a single rounded arch shape (sage or
  apricot) - the series signature. No dashed inner line.
- Logo: top-left, a plain white circle (about 11% of poster width) with a very soft shadow
  and NOTHING inside (the real logo is added afterwards). No other logo or brand name drawn.
- Top-right: small laurel text "alexandramontessori.co.uk".
- Footer: a slim full-width deep laurel bar (about 7% of height), three equal columns, no
  icons, each column = small sage uppercase branch name above white phone number:
    HOUNSLOW 0203 006 5707  |  HESTON 0203 627 6707  |  HAMMERSMITH 0204 618 3477
- Children: high-end UK children's catalogue studio photography, natural and joyful, age 2-4
  (baby where stated), simple knitwear in sage/oat/cream/rust/butter, no logos, no uniforms.
