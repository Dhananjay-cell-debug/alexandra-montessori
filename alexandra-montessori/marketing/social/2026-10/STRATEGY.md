# Alexandra Montessori - Instagram poster strategy (Nov-Dec 2026)

## Who we talk to
Working parents in Hounslow, Heston and Hammersmith (West London), child aged 0-4,
choosing or about to choose a nursery. Diverse community (large South Asian population
in Hounslow/Heston). Searching online, deciding by visit + word of mouth.

## What they worry about (research)
- Cost is the No. 1 barrier (42% "too expensive" - Resonate CX 2025) -> funding content.
- Quality of care is the No. 1 decision factor for many (67% - Good Care Guide).
- First drop-off is one of the most stressful moments of parenthood (12%) -> settling-in.
- Location: two-thirds of London parents want childcare near home / on the commute.
- Montessori is a differentiator but parents don't fully know what it means -> explain it.

## Timely hooks
- Funding code deadline: 31 Dec 2026 for a January 2027 start (apply ~3 weeks early).
- Diwali: Sun 8 Nov 2026 - big moment for our Hounslow/Heston families.
- Winter: coats, cold mornings -> the Montessori coat flip (useful, shareable).

## Content pillars
1. CONVERT - admissions, book a visit, funding deadline.
2. REASSURE - settling in, key person, a day at Alexandra, what to look for on a visit.
3. EXPLAIN / DELIGHT - Montessori made visible, seasonal and cultural moments.
4. RECRUIT - staff.

## Creative principles
- One idea per poster, readable in 2 seconds on a phone; details for those who stop.
- Same brand system on every post (laurel/cream/apricot, arch motif, footer bar) but
  different LAYOUT ideas so the grid never looks like one template.
- Real facts only (site.js / gov.uk). No invented claims, prices or awards.
- Ofsted "Good" only where true (Hounslow, Hammersmith; Heston pending).
- AI children are illustrative - prefer real consented photos when available.
