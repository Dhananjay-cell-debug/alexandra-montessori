# Alexandra Montessori - Halloween poster set (3 posters, 31 Oct 2026)

Finished Instagram posters for Alexandra Montessori, a warm Montessori nursery group in West London
(Hounslow, Heston, Hammersmith). Calm, premium, joyful - like a top UK branding agency. Gentle
Halloween: cosy, curious, NEVER scary. Every word is listed below; add NOTHING else.

## How to work
- Built-in `image_gen`, one call per poster, highest quality, 4:5 portrait (1024x1280 if supported).
- Save each result from `$CODEX_HOME/generated_images/...` into `./full/` using the exact filename
  given (append `-v2` if taken).
- QC at full size: text must match WORD FOR WORD, correct spelling. Check hands (5 fingers),
  faces, and the REAL-PHOTO rules below. If a check fails regenerate (max 2 more tries), keep the best.
- Do nothing else: no code, no other files.

## SHARED RULES (every poster)
- Logo: top-left, ONE plain white circle (~12% of poster width) with a soft shadow and NOTHING
  inside it (the real logo is placed later). Do not draw any logo or brand mark anywhere.
- Top-right, tiny: `alexandramontessori.co.uk`
- Type: chunky, soft, high-contrast display serif for the headline (Cooper / Fraunces Black feel),
  accent line in italic; clean geometric sans (Poppins feel) for small text. Sentence case.
- Margins >= 7%, nothing touching the edges. Keep ALL important text and faces inside the central
  area: nothing essential in the top 8% or bottom 8%.
- At most ONE decorative object besides the photo. No confetti, no all-over patterns, no clip-art
  bats swarm. Minimal and premium.
- COLOUR is NOT locked to the brand: each poster has its own Halloween palette below. Keep the
  white logo circle regardless.

## PHOTO REALISM RULES (children must NOT look AI-generated)
The photograph must look like an unposed documentary photo shot by a nursery professional on a
real camera (35mm, f/2.8, daylight or practical light, light grain, no HDR, no beauty filter).
- Children are absorbed in what they are doing and NOT looking at the camera and NOT posing:
  profile, three-quarter, head tilted down, or partly hidden. Never a row of identical smiles.
- Real skin: pores, flyaway hair, a smudge of paint, slightly uneven blush. No airbrushed, waxy,
  glossy or doll-like faces. Mixed natural expressions (concentrating, mid-giggle, neutral).
- Different heritages and hair types (West London community), ages 2-4 only, simple clothing and
  slightly crumpled homemade-looking costumes. No logos on clothes.
- Exactly five fingers per hand, normal-sized hands, natural grip on props.
- Photo bleeds into the poster background with a soft, organic edge (no hard rectangle).
